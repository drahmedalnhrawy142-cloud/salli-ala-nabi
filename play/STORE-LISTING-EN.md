## Full description
"Salli ala Nabi ﷺ" is a calm app that helps you keep up sending blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ, in a black-and-gold design under a glowing sky.

■ Send blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ
Tap the button and watch the light fall upon the noble shrine. Follow your personal counter and the shared counter of everyone praying with you.

■ Your daily goal
Choose 10, 33, 100, 300 or 1000, or leave it open with no limit (∞), with a day streak and achievement medals.

■ Floating icon and home-screen widget
A small icon over other apps to send blessings any time, and a widget with today's count and a one-tap button.

■ Reminders with a beautiful sound
Reminders at the intervals you choose, with two sound options, plus fixed-time reminders for morning and evening adhkar.

■ Prayer times and Qibla
Prayer times for your city using the Egyptian General Authority of Survey angles, the Qibla direction, and an optional reminder to send blessings after each prayer. No location permission needed.

■ Adhkar, tasbih and multiplied dhikr
Morning, evening, after-prayer and bedtime adhkar with their sources, an electronic tasbih, multiplied dhikr from authentic hadith, and the Beautiful Names of Allah.

■ Statistics and calendar
Weekly and monthly charts, a monthly calendar of your days, medals, a shared collective khatma, and a Friday reminder.

■ The Prophet's seerah
His full lineage, his attributes and character from the two Sahih collections, his names, a timeline of his life, moments from his life, and a knowledge quiz.

■ A card to share
Create a beautiful card with the blessing and your count, add a name as a tribute, and share it in one tap.

■ Arabic and English
Opens in your device language, and you can switch inside the app.

■ Your privacy is kept
No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your data stays on your device.

O Allah, send prayers, peace and blessings upon our master Muhammad.
